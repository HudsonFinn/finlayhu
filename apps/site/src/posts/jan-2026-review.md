January has been a mixed month, it started off exceptionally but has been slightly worse in the last week which will probably tinge this review a little due to recency bias.

I'm still working out the format for this review so I'll likely start it out with a bit of long form content followed by some stat reviews.

---

New years was awesome. A friend was hosting a the party but I had another friend that I brought along. I met a bunch of new people who I liked and have seen a couple of times since.

Other "events" I've had going on this month was a board game night a friend was hosting, planning some stuff for an upcoming trip to iceland, going to a board game venue to play and now I'm on a train to Birmingham to meet a friends new girlfriend. I've also had drinks a few times with work mates including leaving drinks for a coworker.

Climbing has been great this month. We moved from managing to do some V2's to getting multiple V4's which has felt like an incredible jump in skill. Going twice a week has been hard on the body, my fingers are definitely feeling the strain but I think they are getting stronger so hopefully as we continue to go the pain will lessen and we can push further into V4 and onto V5. I would be really happy to get a V5 next month.

Outside of just progress in climbing I'm really happy with how it's effecting my life. I have something to look forward to all the time and we have setup sessions on Mondays and Friday were we have a more chill session on a Monday with more people and then a session on Friday which is more for pushing harder routes. I'd like to keep expanding the people that join us for the Monday sessions and get a proper group going. It would be awesome to build a whole community around it. There are a few people that seem interested so I'll keep expanding it as much as I can.

Running started off really well, I managed to go twice most weeks and 3 times in one. It's been exceptionally hard on my body and not something to look forward to like climbing. There is no social aspect to it and I don't seem to be naturally talented at it. As far as I understand though virtually no one enjoys it when the first start. Initially your body isn't used to the movement so you have to learn that before you become efficient at it and my cardio capacity is almost non-existent after years of doing no exercise. Hopefully as I learn proper form and build cardio capacity it will become more enjoyable, once I am more comfortable running I should be looking to socialise the exercise which will reinforce the behaviour.

On a related note, I've started taking Ramipril again. I have cardiomyopathy (a very mild form) which means that my heart doesn't function quite as well as it should. Ramipril is a ACE inhibitor which reduces blood pressure, therefore taking pressure off the heart. This has seemed to make it slightly harder to run but will benefit me in the long run. I have started on a very low dose and I'm monitoring my blood pressure each morning to make sure it's not getting dangerously low. I will add in Bisoprolol, a beta blocker, next month. This will further increase the difficulty of running I believe as it reduces heart rate and further lowers blood pressure. I will book an appointment to see my new GP to make sure I'm getting the monitoring I need.

I've read 2 books this month, ["In this Economy?" by Kyla Scanlon](https://www.goodreads.com/book/show/150249560-in-this-economy) and ["A twice told therapy" by Dr. Irvin Yalom](https://www.goodreads.com/book/show/21026.Every_Day_Gets_A_Little_Closer), while they were both good they were not exceptional. I've read some of Yalom's other work and enjoyed it much more while Kyla's was just not what I was expecting from someone who's other online work I have enjoyed so much. I also started ["The myth of Sisyphus" by Camus](https://www.goodreads.com/book/show/91950.The_Myth_of_Sisyphus) but actually decided that it wasn't great bedtime reading so switched it out for ["Abundance" by Ezra Klein](https://www.goodreads.com/book/show/91950.The_Myth_of_Sisyphus). There have been mixed reviews for abundance from the left which I consider myself a part of but after reading Kyla's book it seemed to echo elements I've heard in conversations about abundance. These ideas intrigued me. The first few pages (all I've gotten through so far) paint an exciting picture of the future so I'm hopeful for the rest of it.

Overall my bedtimes and wake up times have been good. Theres been a bit of experimentation with it. Initially it was going to bed at the same time and then giving myself more time to sleep if I failed to sleep at the right time. This seemed to work okay for a few days but then I was struggling to fall asleep on time all of the time. I found that actually forcing yourself up at the same time every day no matter how much you slept the night before. This tires you out by the time you go to be and means that you don't spend hours lying in bed but not getting to sleep. I have now set it so that I wake up at 8am every day, no changes on weekends. This seems to be helping even if I get the occasional day of less sleep and therefore less performance.

Work has been fine. Just chugging along. I might have a new cool opportunity coming up in the coming months but I won't talk much about that until it's confirmed. I've been using AI more and thats been cool, I feel like it does increase productivity quite a bit.

Personally I've had a bit of an AI revolution. I've built [chalkboard.fhudson.com](chalkboard.fhudson.com) a design system and then moved my personal site [fhudson.com](fhudson.com) all done almost exclusively with claude code. Pushing this workflow as much as I can personally is helping me keep up with the current state of the art. I'm getting better at prompting and building out workflows. Another part of this is setting up Qin, my locally hosted [OpenClaw](https://openclaw.ai/) bot which I'm experimenting with. At the moment it's pretty cool and has access to my calendar, emails and Oura data. I'm getting it to send me wake up texts on Whatsapp with summaries for the day. I'd like to get it running more independently and going to get it to build a site for itself at qin.fhudson.com which it will own and be able to publish stuff too. I'm having lots of fun with it at the moment and will continue to try and push it to it's limits, I just need to be careful about security.
